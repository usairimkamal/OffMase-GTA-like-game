========================================
 HOW TO PLAY (EDGE / CHROME / ANY)
========================================

Models are now EMBEDDED (base64).
No localhost. No server. No START-GAME.bat needed.

1. Extract the full zip folder
2. Open the folder mini-gta-3d-pack
3. Double-click mini-gta-3d.html
4. Play

Keep these files in the SAME folder:
  - mini-gta-3d.html
  - three.min.js
  - GLTFLoader.js
  - models-data.js   (this is the big one - all 3D models)
  - caricon_phonen1.png
  - textures/ folder

That is all.
